Oche is the dart scorer for your board at home, at the pub or at the club. Enter your darts, Oche does the maths, calls every score like on TV and keeps detailed stats of every game. No account, no sign-up, just open the app and throw.

12 GAME MODES
• X01: 101 to 2001, straight in or double in, double out, master out or single out, with legs and sets
• Cricket and cut-throat cricket, without a round limit
• Around the Clock with jump and doubles-only variants
• Shanghai, Killer, Halve-It and High Score
• Practice: Bob's 27, 121 checkout practice and doubles practice with your hit rate per double
• Party modes: Gotcha and Scram for two players

A REAL CALLER
Real arena atmosphere in your living room: every score called, "You require 141" before the finish, "Game shot, and the leg!" and of course the long, roaring "One hundred and eighty!" with crowd and air horn. Choose a pro voice in English or German, the voice of your phone, or record the complete caller in your own voice. A guided recording studio walks you through every call and every score, starts as soon as you speak and stops by itself.

FAST AND EASY INPUT
Enter your darts the way you like and switch at any time during a game:
• Single darts on a big keypad with double and treble buttons
• Tap the dartboard, with extra-wide treble and double rings so you hit the right field every time
• Visit total: just type the score of your three darts
Mistakes happen, so every dart can be undone. Checkout suggestions show you the best way to finish, with your favourite double.

PLAY AGAINST BOTS
No opponent around? Play against six bot levels, from beginner (average 20–30) to world class (average 85–105). The bots throw like real players with realistic misses, so every game feels different.

ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
Create a room, share the invite link via WhatsApp or any messenger and play together, even when you are in different places. Everyone enters their own darts on their own phone. Up to 8 players and 16 spectators per room. If the host's phone drops out, another device takes over automatically and the game goes on. Online play also works in the free web version, so friends without the app can join from their browser.

TOURNAMENTS
Run your own tournament for the club night or a party: knockout with a bracket, or a league where everyone plays everyone with a full table. Bot-only matches are played in fast mode. Tournaments work online too.

DETAILED STATISTICS
Get better with every game:
• Average history and first-9 average
• 180s, 140+ and 100+ visits, highest finish and best leg
• Checkout rate by score left
• Doubles rate for every single double
• Hit map showing where your darts land
• Head-to-head records against your opponents
• 22 achievements and a new practice challenge every day

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
Big screen mode for tablets and TVs, so the whole room can follow the score. Light and dark theme that follows your phone's settings. Available in 8 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Your games and statistics are stored on your device. Create a backup file at any time and restore it on a new phone.

FREE TO PLAY
Oche is free. A short ad is only shown after a game, never while you are throwing. If you want the app completely ad-free, remove the ads for good with a one-time purchase of €2.99, no subscription.
